Before you choose

  • Visit during class hours and see a normal day.
  • Ask the age rule for each class, and the date on which age is counted.
  • For daycare, ask the exact hours, holidays and who picks up the child.
  • Ask for the full fee in writing — admission, term, transport, uniform.
  • Tap Google Maps on a card for reviews, photos, phone and the route from your home.
