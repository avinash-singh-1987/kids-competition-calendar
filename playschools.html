About this list

🧸

How playschools were chosen

Playschools whose own website or chain website lists them, kindergartens run by the schools on our admissions page, and well-known local playschools that appear in at least two public directories or were suggested by Kochi parents. It is not a complete list of every playschool.

🔎

How to read the cards

From its own website ✓ means we read the details on the playschool's or chain's site. From public listings means directory sites only — please confirm with the playschool. Distances are straight-line; "(area)" means we only know the area, not the exact building.

⚖️

No ratings from us

We never rate or rank playschools. Tap Google Maps on any card for reviews, photos, phone and directions.
