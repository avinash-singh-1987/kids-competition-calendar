Olympiads for Class 1–5: a parent's guide

SOF, SilverZone and Unified Council explained in 5 minutes — who registers, what it costs, when the exams are and how to prepare.

In 30 seconds

  • Three organisers run most Olympiads in Kochi schools: SOF, SilverZone and Unified Council.
  • SOF and SilverZone go through your school. SOF does not accept applications from parents, and SilverZone sends its registration forms to schools — so ask the school early, because it has to register students well before the exam.
  • Unified Council lets parents register directly (school registration not required): the child can take the online exam from home.
  • Most first-round (Level 1) exams are held between September and January.
  • For Class 1 and 2 there is only one round. A second round (Level 2) starts from Class 3 for top scorers.

"Can I register my child myself?"

For SOF, no — SOF says students cannot apply individually; only the school can register them. SilverZone also registers students through schools (its forms are sent to schools), so ask your school first. For Unified Council (NSTSE, UIMO, UIEO), yes — parents can register online and the child writes the exam from home on a computer.

What to ask your school

A short message to the class teacher usually works:

"Is our school taking part in the SOF / SilverZone Olympiads this year? Which date has the school chosen, and by when should we give the name and fee?"

Ask in July–August. The first SOF and SilverZone dates are in late September, and schools often collect names before their own deadline with the organiser.

  • Unified Council NSTSE sample questions ↗ and UIEO sample questions ↗
  • SilverZone past question papers ↗ (PDF downloads on their site).

What a paper looks like for the youngest children (SilverZone iOM, Class 1–2): 25 multiple-choice questions in 40 minutes, no negative marking, English only.

A simple plan for the year

  1. June–August: ask the school which Olympiads it will offer and the last date to give names.
  2. September–December: SOF and SilverZone exams in school.
  3. November–January: Unified Council — register directly online if your school does not take part.
  4. Missed one? Open "Closed for this year" on our calendar and tap Remind me next year — your phone's calendar will remind you in time.
