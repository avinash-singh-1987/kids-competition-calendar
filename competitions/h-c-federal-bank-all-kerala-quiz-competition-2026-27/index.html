For Class 3–7, only through the school (no fee) — ask your school librarian. School-level written test (1 hour, English, multiple choice) was on 30 Oct 2026; the top students of each class went on to the zonal round (5 Dec 2026), then district rounds in early January and the state final at Ernakulam (30 Jan 2027, as listed). Questions are mostly from the 'GK Treasure' book series plus general knowledge and current affairs. A school letter shared online says schools registered by 31 Aug, so ask your school early (June–July) next year.
