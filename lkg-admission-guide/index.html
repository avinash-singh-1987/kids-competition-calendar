LKG admission in Kochi: a parent's guide

Age rules, when each school takes applications, documents to keep ready — in 5 minutes.

In 30 seconds

  • Most popular Kochi schools take children into LKG at 4 to under 5 years of age, counted on 1 June (some use 31 May; Kendriya Vidyalayas use 31 March).
  • Applications open 8–11 months before the June start — for most schools between July and October of the year before.
  • Some schools give seats first-come, first-served — apply as soon as applications open.
  • Keep the birth certificate ready. Rajagiri's notice says applications with any date-of-birth mismatch are rejected.

Documents to keep ready

  • Birth certificate — asked by every school (Rajagiri wants the page showing the child's name, date of birth and parents' names).
  • Passport-size photo of the child (Rajagiri, Mar Thoma).
  • Immunisation / vaccination card (Mar Thoma, Delhi Public School).
  • Aadhaar — of the child, and at some schools also the parents (Mar Thoma, Delhi Public School).

How schools choose

  • First-come, first-served: Mar Thoma says KG seats are given mainly in order of application (siblings get preference).
  • A friendly interaction, not a test: Mar Thoma and Delhi Public School (no entrance test up to Grade 4); TIPS says there is no entrance exam for the early years.
  • Strict age check: Rajagiri rejects applications that don't meet its age rule.

Never miss the date

On the school admissions page, each school card has a Remind me button: it puts a reminder in your phone's calendar for the month that school usually opens applications.
