    About this calendar

    🔎

    Where the information comes from

    Official Olympiad websites, Kochi newspapers in English and Malayalam, and education blogs. We check for new competitions every week, and finished competitions are hidden automatically.

    🏷️

    How to read the labels

    Confirmed ✓ means the dates came from the organiser. Not yet confirmed means they came from a news report or blog. Every card links to its source, so you can check it yourself.

    ⚖️

    An independent, free guide

    This calendar is not connected to SOF, SilverZone, any school or any newspaper. Always confirm dates and fees with your school or the organiser before you register or pay.
